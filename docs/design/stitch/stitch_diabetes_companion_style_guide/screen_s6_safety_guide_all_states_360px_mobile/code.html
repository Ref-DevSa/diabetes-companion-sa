In an emergency, get help now

Call 112 from a cell phone or 10177 for an ambulance, or go to the nearest hospital. Do not wait for this app.

Symptoms alone cannot confirm diabetes. Only a test at a clinic can.

Signs of low blood sugar

  • Shaking or feeling jittery
  • Sweating
  • Sudden hunger
  • Feeling dizzy or confused
  • A fast heartbeat

Signs of high blood sugar

  • Feeling very thirsty
  • Passing urine more often
  • Blurred eyesight
  • Feeling very tired
  • Feeling sick (nausea)

Get urgent medical help if…

  • The person is unconscious or cannot be woken
  • They are very confused or cannot speak clearly
  • They have a fit (seizure)
  • They breathe fast and deeply, or keep vomiting
  • You are not sure. It is always safer to call.
