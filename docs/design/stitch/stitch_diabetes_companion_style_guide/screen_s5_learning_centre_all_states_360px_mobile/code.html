What is diabetes?

The basics in plain words

3 min read Reading now

Diabetes is when your body has too much sugar (called glucose) in the blood.

Normally, a hormone called insulin helps move sugar from food into your body's cells to give you daily energy. In diabetes, either the body does not make enough insulin, or the insulin cannot work properly.

Plain truth: With healthy everyday meals, regular walks, your clinic medicine, and checking your blood sugar, you can live a full, strong life.

High blood sugar over a long time can hurt your eyes, feet, and heart. That is why simple daily care matters so much.
