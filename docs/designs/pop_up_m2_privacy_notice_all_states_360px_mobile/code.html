Pop-up M2 “Privacy Notice” — Diabetes Companion SA

Purpose: Provide clear, transparent POPIA & health data privacy terms. Accessible by anyone (authenticated or unauthenticated, e.g. from Sign up, Log in, or Settings). Built as a scrollable modal overlay with dimmed background on a 360 px viewport. Includes explicit headings, strict verbatim copy, and an accessible 48 px high "Close" button.

Frame 1: Normal (Scrolled to Top) Frame 2: Normal (Scrolled to Bottom + Close button in view) Frame 3: Offline Mode (Works as normal, identical cached experience)

Privacy Notice

What we collect

Your email address, the glucose readings, notes and checklist items you save, your language choice, and the dates you agreed to this notice and confirmed you are 18 or older.

Why we collect it

To let you log in and keep your own diary and checklist. We do not sell your information or use it for adverts.

Who can see it

Only you. The app’s administrator can see your information in the database only when needed to fix a fault.

Where it is stored

In a database run by Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany, which is outside South Africa. Sign-up emails are sent through Brevo.
